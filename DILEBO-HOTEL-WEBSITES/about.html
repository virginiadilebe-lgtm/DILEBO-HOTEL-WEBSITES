About Dilebo Hotel

Our Story

Established in 2018, Dilebo Hotel has been providing warm African hospitality in the heart of Polokwane, Limpopo. Our name "Dilebo" means "journey" in Sepedi, and we are committed to making every guest's journey comfortable and memorable.

Our Mission

To provide affordable luxury accommodation with exceptional service, while showcasing the culture and beauty of Limpopo Province to all our visitors.

Our Facilities

  • Free High-Speed WiFi
  • 24-Hour Front Desk & Security
  • Restaurant & Bar
  • Conference Facilities
  • Free Parking
  • Swimming Pool
